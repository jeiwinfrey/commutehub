# CommuteHub illustration prompts

Every illustration is generated from the **Global style** below plus one **Asset prompt**.
Always send them together so the set stays consistent.

## Global style (prepend to every prompt)

Flat vector illustration, minimalist. Solid color fills only: no gradients, no shadows, no outlines or strokes, no textures, no 3D.
Palette: sky blue #00A6FF, green #00CC3F, light gray #F2F2F7, white #FFFFFF, and a mid gray #8D8D8E for small details. Pure white background.
Soft rounded geometric shapes, friendly proportions, faceless simple characters, generous empty space, centered composition, square 1:1 canvas.
Setting: Ilocos Norte, Philippines (jeepneys, tricycles, rice fields, windmills, Paoay church silhouette) kept very simple.
